Rover Competition

Project Director for Team Rovus, managing rover development and leading mechanical design efforts for Mars rover competitions.

Project Description

As Project Director for Team Rovus (Robotique UdeS), I manage our Mars rover development for the Canadian International Rover Challenge. My responsibilities include overseeing the mechanical team, designing and iterating on robotic arm systems, and working on materials selection for our rocker-bogie suspension and wheel designs. I also contribute hands-on to electrical work, including PCB assembly and circuit debugging, ensuring all systems integrate properly for competition.

Over the past few years with Team Rovus, I've balanced project management with technical work. We placed 5th at CIRC in 2023 and 2024, and achieved 1st place in 2024 and 3rd place in 2025 at CRQRC. The project involves coordinating between mechanical, electrical, and software teams while personally contributing to design work and troubleshooting. It's been a good learning experience in both technical skills and team leadership.

Key Points

📊

Project Management

Directed Team Rovus through multiple competition cycles, coordinating between different engineering teams and managing project timelines. Results include 5th place at CIRC (2023 & 2024) and podium finishes at CRQRC (1st in 2024, 3rd in 2025).

🦾

Robotic Arm Development

Designed and built multiple iterations of robotic arms for the rover, focusing on weight, precision and overall cost. Worked on joint mechanisms, actuator and gearbox selection, and control integration to meet tasks requirements.

⚡

Mechanical & Electrical Work

Led the mechanical team while also working on electrical systems. Contributed to rocker-bogie design, wheel material selection, PCB assembly, and circuit debugging. Gained experience bridging mechanical and electrical engineering aspects of the project.
